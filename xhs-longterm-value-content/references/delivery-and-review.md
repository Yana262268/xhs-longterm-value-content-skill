# Delivery and review

## Suggested project layout

project/
├── research/
│   ├── benchmark-notes.md
│   └── selection-log.md
├── copy/
│   ├── publish-caption.txt
│   └── product-cards.md
├── assets/
│   ├── source/             # links or user-provided files; avoid redistributing restricted assets
│   └── final/
│       ├── 00_cover.png
│       ├── 01_product.png
│       └── …
├── review.md
└── publish-images.zip

Keep a separate sources.md or selection log when the user needs traceability. Keep the upload ZIP limited to final images unless the user asks for source records too.

## Final review

Use this checklist:

### Research

- [ ] Public benchmark sources have URLs, checked dates, and why they mattered.
- [ ] Trend claims are labeled as signals, not universal truths.
- [ ] Product facts came from official or user-approved sources.
- [ ] Current price/availability caveats are recorded outside graphics.

### Selection

- [ ] Six products meet palette rules.
- [ ] Each has a distinct use need.
- [ ] Each has a concrete low-budget/high-return explanation.
- [ ] No hard-to-isolate or cluttered source image remains.

### Copy

- [ ] Title matches the brief exactly.
- [ ] Product cards use the account’s information density and voice.
- [ ] Copy is original and avoids unverified personal experiences.
- [ ] Caption is ready to paste.

### Visuals

- [ ] Cover is readable in thumbnail view.
- [ ] Product cards follow one consistent visual system.
- [ ] Product-only cards contain no internal notes or source labels.
- [ ] Export dimensions and file names are correct.

## Completion note

Report:

- selected products and the selection logic;
- what was updated in the visual system;
- file links to the caption, source log, final folder, and ZIP;
- known limits such as approximate exchange conversions or an unresolved named inspiration source.

Do not publish, send messages, or claim external posting was completed unless the user explicitly authorized and the action succeeded.
