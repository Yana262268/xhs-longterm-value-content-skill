# Visual production

## Asset source policy

Use assets in this order:

1. Brand official product page or official flagship-store asset.
2. Retailer/manufacturer page approved by the user.
3. User-provided image.
4. AI-generated image only when the user explicitly requests an illustrative substitute.

For official-product posts, preserve the actual product. Do not generate a replacement product, silently alter its color, or fabricate packaging. Remove the background only; retain logos, proportions, material details, and all distinctive product geometry.

## Cutout readiness

Choose products that can produce a clean, consistent card:

- clear edge against background or transparent official PNG;
- simple silhouette without a person, hand, crowded scene, complex reflection, or occlusion;
- enough native resolution for the target post size;
- color that works with the other five items.

If the original product image contains a complex scene, find another official asset. Do not force a low-quality cutout into a polished six-product layout.

## Six-product cover

Default canvas: 1080 × 1440 px (3:4). Adapt only when the target platform requires another size.

For a six-product account cover:

- Use a solid background chosen from the brief’s palette.
- Place products in a 3-over-3 grid with consistent visual boxes. Align by visual mass, not raw pixel bounds.
- Scale products generously enough to fill their cells while leaving a clear central title zone.
- Use a large, readable headline. If the title is long, break it at a semantic boundary into two or three lines.
- Keep the title in the center and product rows balanced above and below it.
- Do not add frames, source lines, decorative stickers, labels, or unrelated shapes unless they appear in the account’s samples.
- Inspect at feed-thumbnail scale: title must remain readable and the six-product idea must be immediately visible.

## Single-product cards

Default canvas: 1080 × 1440 px. Keep all cards structurally identical:

- white or sample-matched simple background;
- large centered isolated product;
- one line: 产品名 价格;
- 2–4 centered body lines;
- no URLs, source credits, disclaimers, watermarks, or additional labels in the visual;
- consistent type sizes and vertical positions across the six cards.

Put price/source caveats in the caption or source log, never in the product-card design unless the user asks.

## Visual QA

Check before delivery:

- all canvases have the same dimensions;
- six objects are consistently scaled on the cover;
- no background residue remains around cutouts;
- no title overlaps an object;
- text fits without truncation;
- selected products match the requested palette;
- product colors and branded details match official assets;
- cover has no accidental decorative elements when a pure background was requested.
