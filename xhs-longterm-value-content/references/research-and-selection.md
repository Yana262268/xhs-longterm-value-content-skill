# Research and selection

## Research lanes

Use at least two lanes when the user asks for current or trend-led selection.

| Lane | Purpose | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account history | Establish the account’s recurring categories, price bands, language, image density, and audience expectation | Note URL/sample date, title, engagement visible in the sample, and repeatable pattern |
| Xiaohongshu public search | Find relevant high-engagement notes and accounts under topic terms | Keyword, note/account URL, publish date, visible engagement, products/categories, hook, and why it is useful |
| Douyin public search | Find faster-moving price, use-case, or purchase-friction framing | Query, URL, date if available, framing pattern, and transferable insight |
| Public trend/hot-list sources | Detect rising categories and language | Source URL, checked date, indicator used, and limitation |
| Official product pages | Verify exact product, color, image, current price, and availability | Brand, product URL, market/currency, checked date, product image URL if used |

Use the user’s requested channels first. If a named source/account cannot be resolved from public search, record it as unverified rather than guessing; continue with the other confirmed sources.

## Suggested search vocabulary

Start with the account’s exact niche, then combine one value term with one audience or scene term:

- 长期主义单品 / 长期主义女大 / 长期主义买包
- 花小钱装大杯 / 花小钱办大事 / 反向消费 / 理性消费
- 女大品质好物 / 通勤好物 / 上课好物 / 图书馆好物
- 平价高级感 / 显贵感 / 使用频率高 / 一物多用
- color + product category, such as 粉紫色系 包 / 浅紫耳机 / 粉色通勤包

Search results are candidate signals, not facts. Prioritize recency, visible engagement, fit with the account’s audience, repeated appearance across independent creators, and availability of verifiable products.

## Candidate scorecard

Score each candidate 1–5. Do not select an item merely because it scores high on a single dimension.

| Dimension | What earns a high score |
| --- | --- |
| Account fit | Matches the account’s category, price sensitivity, tone, and audience |
| Value proof | Clear high-frequency use, multiple scenes, or a credible wardrobe/device upgrade |
| Visual fit | Correct palette, clean silhouette, easy official cutout, and good balance with other items |
| Information certainty | Exact model/color/price and official image are available |
| Freshness | Supported by current public conversation or demand signals |
| Diversity | Adds a different need state without repeating another item |

For a six-item post, aim for 3–4 categories rather than six near-identical items. A practical mix can include carry, wear, device, hydration, and audio; change it when the account or topic demands otherwise.

## “花小钱装大杯” proof test

For every selected product, write one defensible answer to each question:

1. What does the buyer spend? Use an exact or clearly approximate price.
2. What feels upgraded? Silhouette, color harmony, brand access point, organization, or a daily ritual.
3. Where does it get used? Name at least two realistic scenes when possible.
4. Why does it reduce waste? It serves several outfits/scenes, has a high use frequency, or replaces a weaker stopgap purchase.

Reject generic claims such as “quality is amazing,” “luxury feeling,” or “worth it” unless source-backed and explained in terms of observable use or design.
