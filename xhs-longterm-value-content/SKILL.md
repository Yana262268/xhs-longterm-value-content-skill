# Xiaohongshu Long-Term Value Content

Produce a complete Xiaohongshu product-recommendation post that makes a low-budget, high-perceived-value proposition concrete. The output is a researched, visual-ready content package: selection reasoning, a cover, six product cards, a post caption, source records, and a concise evaluation.

Use this skill for public, consumer-product content. Do not use it to imply sponsorship, invent product performance, scrape private data, or publish to an account without explicit authorization.

## Read the brief first

Extract or request only the information that affects the result:

- Account link/ID or 3–10 representative notes/screenshots.
- Topic, audience, palette, product count, price range, geographic market, and desired post format.
- Any fixed selection rules, brand exclusions, and source preferences.
- Whether the user wants research only, copy only, visual assets, or the complete package.

When some inputs are absent, state practical assumptions and begin with research. Do not block ordinary work waiting for a complete brief.

## Workflow

1. Build an account style profile from the user’s samples. Read references/style-and-copy.md.
2. Research fresh public demand signals and benchmark content. Read references/research-and-selection.md.
3. Select products using the value and visual criteria; verify model, color, price, and image source through official pages.
4. Draft the title, post caption, and single-product copy. Each item must articulate a real “花小钱装大杯” value mechanism.
5. Produce the cover and product cards according to references/visual-production.md.
6. Save a source log, a selection decision table, final assets, and the publish-ready caption. Read references/delivery-and-review.md.

## Required decision rules

- **Source hierarchy:** account samples and public benchmark notes establish content language; public trend/hot-list sources provide current angles; official brand pages verify products. Keep these roles separate.
- **Recent research:** when “recent” is requested, use the last 14 calendar days unless the user sets another range. Record publishing date, engagement signal, URL, author/account, and why the note was useful.
- **Public only:** use public pages and lawful tool access. Never claim complete platform coverage.
- **Original wording:** analyze hooks, value logic, structure, and information density. Write new copy; do not reproduce benchmark sentences.
- **Price integrity:** show price with market/currency and source date. Mark approximate exchange conversions as approximate. Recheck prices before posting.
- **Value integrity:** “花小钱装大杯” means a visible return on budget, such as high use frequency, multiple scenes, better integration with existing clothes/devices, or a lower entry point to a desired aesthetic. It does not mean labeling every inexpensive product as good value.
- **Visual integrity:** prefer official clean product images. Remove background while retaining the actual product’s color, proportions, branding, and distinctive details. Do not substitute AI-invented products when an official image is required.
- **Palette integrity:** include only products that work together at first glance. Reject colors, styles, or image shapes that break the defined palette or cannot be cleanly isolated.

## Final deliverables

Unless the brief narrows scope, deliver:

- research/benchmark-notes.md — current platform findings and linked sources.
- research/selection-log.md — candidates, selection decision, official verification links, price date, and exclusions.
- copy/publish-caption.txt — title, caption, product list, hashtags.
- copy/product-cards.md — the text that appears on each card.
- assets/final/ — numbered cover and product-card images, ready for upload.
- review.md — checklist result and unresolved claims or price caveats.
- a ZIP containing only publish-ready image assets, plus a separate project/source package when requested.

Keep user-facing product cards clean. Do not put source URLs, scraping notices, price caveats, or internal process text on the visuals unless requested.

## Scope changes

If the user corrects palette, density, headline, image source, product count, or wording, treat that as an update to the working creative brief. Revise all affected deliverables and re-check the cover and at least two product pages before sending the final package.
